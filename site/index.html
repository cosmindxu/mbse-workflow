Drone-swarm model

This is the surveillance drone-swarm model from mbse-workflow: a layered, SysML-like model written layer by layer by LLM agents and gated at every step by a deterministic checker. It opens in Sysprose, a browser modeller, where every view of the model — general, interconnection, action, state, requirement, tree, tables, 3D — is drawn from the one text file.

Open the model

New to it? Seven short video tutorials — silent, captioned — walk through every diagram, one layer at a time, and show how to add an element.

Anyone can change the model in the browser: edits stay in that browser until they are saved or exported. Changes reach the repository through GitHub, under its usual permissions — see proposing a change to the model. The modeller needs JavaScript; the model's text does not.

Sysprose reads and writes a SysML v2–style notation. It is a candidate, not a certified or conformance-tested implementation, and the model is spec-shaped, not conformant.
