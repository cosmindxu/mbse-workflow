# S10

## What was decided

Started from the given OA draft, which was already structurally complete (all 13 brief-named coordination/C2 action defs present and tagged, the untagged `watchAsset` population entity, two representatives `assetAlpha`/`assetBravo` each with their own allocated activities and nine inter-member flows, eight `#Capability` use case defs with subjects, a `#Mission` use case def, and a local `Hazards` package with one hazard stated and satisfied). That draft, however, contained exactly the mistake the brief warns about hardest: the two human-actor `#Actor part def`s (SwarmSupervisor, OperationsCentreAnalyst) had their activities declared as `action` usages nested *inside* the part def body, and six flows (missionTaskingFlow, supervisoryCommandFlow, recallFlow, statusPictureFlow, detectionReportFlow, ackFlow) then reached into those nested actions with three-segment ends (`swarmSupervisor.taskSurveillanceMission.cmdOut`, `opsCentreAnalyst.reportDetection.reportIn`, etc.) -- exactly the `ref/unresolved-flow-end` pattern the task calls out, since a three-segment end resolves to nothing once this layer is carried down. Fixed by: pulling every actor activity out to a package-level `action` usage beside its actor's part (`supervisorTaskSurveillanceMission`, `supervisorIssueSupervisoryCommand`, `supervisorRecallToLand`, `supervisorPresentStatusPicture`, `analystReportDetection`, `analystAcknowledgeDetectionReport`), giving each its own doc, allocating each to its actor with a bare `allocate`, and rewriting the six flows to use two-segment `usage.port` ends throughout, matching the pattern already correctly used for the inter-member flows. Everything else (resources, action defs, member population, modes, capabilities, mission, hazard) was kept because it already satisfied every checked criterion: the two tagged state defs (`#Mode`/`#State`) on `WatchAssetMember` are both fully reachable from `start`; every operational activity (member and actor) now has an `allocate` statement; every flow now resolves at both ends inside this package; no element is named like the system; the population entity is declared under the exact untagged name `watchAsset`; and the one OA-added hazard is stated once and satisfied by path within this same layer, so nothing here is left unmitigated.

## Deliberately left for later

- The brief's member-owned degraded modes (NavigationDegraded, SensingDegraded, Isolated, Quarantined) and the fleet-owned MeshDegraded are not instantiated as states here: they describe conditions of the concrete mesh-radio-carrying SurveillanceDrone member and its fleet, which CV-16 introduces only from LA down. OA's WatchAssetOperatingMode/WatchAssetAvailabilityState give the generic, fully reachable base machine (#Mode and #State, per the option) that LA is expected to refine with those named states when it declares SurveillanceDrone and the fleet population.
- The CV-18 rule state-machine annotations (RecallWins, ReturnsWhenIsolated, GeofenceBreachEndsWatch, QuarantinedStaysOut) and the brief's full hazard list (PositionDriftHazard, MeshJammingHazard, MisclassificationHazard, SensingDegradedUnnoticedHazard, StaleTrackHazard, SpoofedMemberHazard, MixedClassifierVersionsHazard, OperatorOverloadHazard) are explicitly stated by the brief to live in SA::Hazards and be satisfied from SA down; not modeled at OA, which only adds and satisfies its own CoverageGapDuringHandoverHazard.
- GroundStation, GroundStationLink, RechargePointAndBatterySwap, RecoveryPoint, StoredMaps and ClassifierRelease remain declared as passive resource parts with docs but without explicit OA-level flows/connections into them; concrete wiring (e.g. a member's link to groundStationLink, or its query against storedMaps during NavigationDegraded) is left for LA/PA where ports and connections on the concrete SurveillanceDrone are defined.
